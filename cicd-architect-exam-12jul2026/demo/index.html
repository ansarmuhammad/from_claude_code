Some jobs take a while. Instead of making you wait, the front desk hands the job to a separate worker and gets back to you right away. A messenger called Redis carries the job from one to the other.

Step 7

🧰 Working Quietly in the Background

Two more pieces are running the whole time, even though they don't have a webpage of their own.

Redis — this is the messenger you already saw carry your job to the worker in Step 3. It's doing real work above, right now.
PostgreSQL — a proper long-term filing cabinet. The to-do list from Step 2 is genuinely saved in here now, not just remembered in memory — restart the API and your tasks will still be there.
Everything above is exactly what docker-compose.yml starts, exactly what docs/architecture.md describes,
and exactly what .github/workflows/ci-cd-pipeline.yml builds, tests, and ships automatically on every change.
